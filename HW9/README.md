# HW9 — Neural Networks

**Goal:** Apply neural networks to classification and regression, and compare them with classical models.

- **Classification – breast cancer:** MLP classifiers (scikit-learn and PyTorch).
- **Regression – bike-sharing demand (`day.csv`):** date feature engineering and MLP regressors for daily rental counts.
- Conceptual questions: why neural networks are powerful, and what makes designing them hard.

Notebook: [`HW9_Neural_Networks.ipynb`](HW9_Neural_Networks.ipynb) · Data: `breast-cancer.csv`, `day.csv`
